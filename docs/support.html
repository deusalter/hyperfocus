Help and support

Something wrong, or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. It helps to say what you tapped and which iPhone and iOS version you have.

Please don't paste your calendar link, since it can be personal to you.

The timer ended and I didn't get an alert

Hyperfocus asks for permission the first time you start a session. If you said no, go to Settings → Notifications → Hyperfocus and turn on Allow Notifications. Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can also hold alerts back.

How do I connect Canvas?

In Canvas, open Calendar and tap Calendar Feed, usually at the bottom of the page, then copy the link. In Hyperfocus, go to You → Import → Connect and paste it.

Assignments due in the next 30 days wait in Later, then join Today on the day they're due. Anything already past is ignored, so connecting never opens with a backlog. Anything you finish or let go stays gone.

Google Calendar and Outlook have similar links, usually called the secret or public iCal address. Repeating events aren't expanded yet, so you may only see one-off events from those.

I can't hear the focus sound

Sounds play only while a session is running, so check it isn't paused and that you've picked one under You → Focus sound or from the pill at the top of the focus screen. They use your media volume, and they mix with other audio rather than stopping it, so another app may be louder.

What happens when the timer runs out?

Your minutes are saved and the app asks whether you finished. "Not yet" keeps the task on your list, and you can go another round straight away. Only you decide a task is done.

How do points work?

Every finished thing earns a fixed amount for having started, plus points for focused minutes. Minutes count at full value up to 25, then at half. So a long session still earns the most of any single task, without being able to outweigh a day made of several starts.

What's the "typical day"?

The middle of your recent active days. It's deliberately never your best day, so one big Tuesday can't become a bar you're stuck trying to clear.

The comparisons stress me out

Turn on You → Gentle mode. It removes the race field, day counts and week-over-week comparisons everywhere.

Can I stop the reminders?

The daily nudge is off until you turn it on, under You → Reminders. It sends one a day at most, skips any day you've already started something, and goes quiet on its own if you've been away for a week.

How do I move to a new phone?

Your data lives only on your phone, so it moves with an iCloud or computer backup and restore. You → Export my data gives you a copy as text, but there's no import for it yet.

Can I capture without opening the app first?

Yes. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with the Open URLs action and the address hyperfocus://dump. It opens straight to a brain dump. Add it to your Home Screen, or assign it to the Action button.

How do I delete everything?

You → Reset everything, or delete the app. There's no account and nothing held anywhere else. More in the privacy policy.
