Hyperfocus

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version: Hyperfocus has no accounts and no servers. Your tasks stay on your phone, and we never receive them. We don't collect, sell or share any of your data, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app.

What the app stores

Everything you put into Hyperfocus is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your tasks, first steps and due dates
  • your history of finished tasks and focus sessions, which is how points and charts are calculated
  • your settings, including the name you chose to be called
  • the calendar link, if you connect one

None of this is sent to us. We have no way to see it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device to tell you when a focus session ends, and to send a daily nudge if you turn that on. They are created on your phone. The app doesn't use a push notification server and doesn't request a push token.

Calendar links

Connecting a calendar is optional. If you paste in a link, such as your Canvas calendar feed, your phone downloads that calendar directly from the service it points to. It doesn't pass through us. That service sees an ordinary web request from your device, including your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The app checks the link again when you open it, at most once every six hours, and not at all while it's closed. Anything imported is stored on your phone like the rest of your tasks.

A calendar link can be personal to you, so treat it like a password. It stays on your phone, and removing it is one tap on the You tab.

Lock Screen and widgets

A running session shows on your Lock Screen, and the optional widget shows the next task on your list. Both are drawn on your phone from data that never leaves it. They do mean a task's title can be seen without unlocking the phone. You can turn the Lock Screen display off under Settings → Hyperfocus → Live Activities, and the widget only exists if you add it.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device may include the app's data in them, under Apple's terms and your own settings. That's handled by your device and Apple, and we can't access it.

What Apple may share with us

If you've agreed to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and aggregate statistics, such as install counts. These can't be used to identify you and don't include anything you've entered in the app.

Web pages

The Help and Privacy links open pages hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs for those visits under its own privacy statement. We don't add any analytics to them.

Deleting your data

Everything can be erased with You → Reset everything, or by deleting the app. Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to delete and no account to close. You → Export my data lets you take a copy of your tasks and history first, and Restore from an export reads one back in. Both happen on your phone.

Children

Hyperfocus isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting anything, this policy and the App Store privacy label will both say so first.
