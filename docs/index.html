Hyperfocus

Race your past self, gently.

An iPhone app for ADHD brains, made by someone with one. Miss a day and nothing breaks.

Empty your head. Type whatever's rattling around, then sort it later with a swipe.

Start one thing. The app picks the task, because choosing is its own kind of hard. One tap begins a focus session.

Pass your recent days. Your last several active days run on the track with you. The target is a typical day, never your best one.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
